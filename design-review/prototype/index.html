The world AI agents

Perplexity launches its own take on AI agents for work

Always-on “Teammates” that hold a role inside your workspace, and carry the projects, skills and memory you already have in Perplexity's tools.

  • What happened

    Perplexity shipped a work-focused agent product that assigns agents to roles and lets them reuse context from its search and computer tools.

  • Why it matters

    Agents are moving from a chat box into a shared workspace. The hard questions are no longer “can it act?” but “who owns its memory, and who audits what it did?”

  • In class

    COMP 340 · Software Engineering: a teammate agent is a service with state. Ask students to define its contract, its audit log, and its failure mode.
